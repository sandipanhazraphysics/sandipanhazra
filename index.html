About Me

Research • Innovation • Quantum Technologies

I am currently pursuing my PhD in Physics at the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, where I work in Quantum Information Theory. My research is centered on understanding fundamental aspects of quantum systems while developing tools for quantum information processing and quantum technologies.

My current interests include Discrete-Time Quantum Walks, quantum correlations, multipartite entanglement, quantum communication, quantum algorithms, and quantum computing. I am particularly interested in how quantum walks can be utilized for quantum information processing and many-body quantum systems.

Beyond research, I enjoy scientific discussions, mentoring students, science communication, and developing efficient computational methods for large-scale quantum simulations.
