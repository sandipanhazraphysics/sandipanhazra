About Me

I am currently a PhD Scholar in the Department of Physics at IIT Kharagpur, working on Quantum Information Theory. Before beginning my doctoral studies, I completed my M.Sc. in Physics at IIT Kharagpur, graduating as the Institute Silver Medalist, and my B.Sc. (Hons.) in Physics at Jadavpur University, where I graduated as the Gold Medalist of my department.

My interests lie at the intersection of quantum information, quantum metrology and sensing, quantum computing, and quantum technology more broadly — along with a genuine enthusiasm for science communication.

Relevant courses: Quantum Information, Photonic Quantum Information Technology, Quantum Optics, Quantum Many‑Body Theory, Quantum Sensing and Metrology.
